Sweet Maple

One of San Francisco's most iconic brunch spots with rich, indulgent flavors.

Why I Love Sweet Maple

Sweet Maple is one of those places that feels like a must-try if you're in San Francisco. I usually come here when I'm craving something really indulgent and filling rather than a light brunch.

What makes this place stand out is their famous Millionaire's Bacon — it's sweet, crispy, and honestly one of the best things on the menu. Everything here feels rich and comforting, which makes it perfect for treating yourself.

The portions are also pretty big, so you definitely leave feeling full. It's a great spot to go with friends, especially when you're in the mood for a more "classic brunch" experience.

It does get super busy, especially on weekends, but honestly the food makes it worth the wait.

Pros

  • Famous signature dishes
  • Rich and satisfying food
  • Large portions
  • Great brunch experience

Cons

  • Long wait times
  • Expensive
  • Very crowded on weekends

Menu: Millionaire's bacon, French toast, eggs benedict, pancakes

Price: $20–30
